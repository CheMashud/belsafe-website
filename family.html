BelSafe Family: how it works

BelSafe warns Belgian seniors about scam calls. Family is a scam alert, not a monitoring tool: it reports that a number already on our fraud list called, and nothing about what the person does.

What a relative receives

Only when a number on our fraud list calls the person, and our server has checked that it is on the list:

  • the reported number, with the date and time;
  • how strongly it was reported;
  • the name the relative uses for the person.

The relative also sees that the person accepted, and when, with the first letter of their e-mail address and the part after the @.

What a relative never receives

  • Location
  • Contacts, messages or the rest of the call history
  • Any call from a number that is not on the list. Calls from the person's contacts give no message either, when contacts access is on
  • Call audio
  • Installed apps, or the state of the phone

BelSafe does not request permission for location, SMS, the call log or the microphone. It does not use an accessibility service, and it does not use QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES. Contacts are read only on the phone, so that their calls come through as normal, and are never sent anywhere.

Nothing happens without the person

The person accepts on their own phone, on a screen that names the relative and lists what is shared, with Yes and No. A relative cannot add anyone. An invitation works once and expires after 24 hours.

A relative cannot request information, cannot change a setting and cannot control the phone in any way.

Always visible, easy to stop

  • The home screen shows who receives messages.
  • The Family tab shows which messages were sent in the last 30 days.
  • BelSafe sends a reminder every 30 days, if notifications are on.
  • The person can stop in three taps, per relative. Those alerts are removed from the relative's list at once.

Alerts are kept for 30 days. A person can have at most five relatives, and a relative can help at most four people.

Not hidden, not marketed for tracking

BelSafe has a fixed icon and name and never hides itself. Family is described in the store listing. We do not market it as a way to follow anyone, so BelSafe does not declare isMonitoringTool. We do not sell data and show no advertising.
